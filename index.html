SLFRF Project Planning Guide

A guide for program staff to determine a project's eligibility and expenditure category, and to identify its reporting requirements before agreements are executed or funds are obligated.

Built under the Interim Final Rule, 2021 Demo
01 · Find the category

Start from the statutory use

Pick the eligible use closest to the project's scope, then the expenditure category. The category sets the reporting: project demographic distribution (PDD), evidence-based interventions (EBI), programmatic data, and for water, sewer and broadband, infrastructure detail.

04 · The implementation guidance

The control the guide sits inside

The planning guide was one exhibit to a written policy and implementation guidance, the recipient's key control for complying with SLFRF. Its framework:

Purpose

What SLFRF is, the recipient's responsibility for compliant use and documentation, and the guidance's scope: SLFRF only, not ARPA funds received through other federal programs.

Governance

The appropriation trail from the governing body's approvals, the initial funding allocations aligned to the statutory uses, reprogrammed funds, and each internal and external stakeholder's role.

Planning and early implementation

A finance, grants and budget management team meeting weekly, widened to program staff and project managers; grants management leads drawdowns and Treasury reporting, with portal access limited to trained staff.

Key control updates

The authorities the guidance tracks: the statute, the Interim and Final Rules and their FAQs, Treasury's compliance and reporting guidance, the Uniform Guidance, and the recipient's own grant policies.

Program administration

Administrative decisions (the revenue loss calculation, a dedicated fund, project fields for expenditure categories), this planning guide and the request form, a continuous training program, quarterly reporting, and subrecipient monitoring.
